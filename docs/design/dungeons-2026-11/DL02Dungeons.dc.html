Dungeons tab

1Always every dungeon, grouped Fits you · Higher · Lower for the character you’re logged in on (Hex 16). No picker, no checkbox. Fits = your level is at most 2 below the minimum and not above the maximum. Lower only appears when it has dungeons.
2Wings are their own entries, indented under the dungeon name (Scarlet Monastery: Graveyard, Library…), like runs already name them.
3Detail top to bottom: name, level, zone, fits · Last cleared by … · a planned run (opens it in Runs) · Ask now · Plan a run… → Bosses & loot → Quests (the AG05 list).
4Loot rows: icon with quality border, name in quality colour, type in grey, drop % right-aligned, highest first per boss. Hover = the game’s own GameTooltip:SetItemByID. Shift-click links it in chat.
5Not usable by your class (armor type, weapon skill, class-only) is dimmed to 45 %. Usable by Hex hides them; remembered per character.
6Data: boss order from the client’s DungeonEncounter table, loot from cmangos, shipped in the addon as a data file per release.
7Cleared = the dungeon’s final boss died (The Deadmines: Edwin VanCleef). Each dungeon has one final boss and may have optional bosses (Cookie), kept in a small hand-made list in the import. “Last cleared by” names the friends in that group.
8Which loot: only the boss’s own drops at 1 % or more, highest first; shared world-drop tables are left out. Quest items and recipe drops from the boss’s own table always show, even under 1 %. Max 6 per boss, then a “2 more ▾” row that expands in place (counted after the Usable switch).
9Raids are not in this version: dungeons only.
