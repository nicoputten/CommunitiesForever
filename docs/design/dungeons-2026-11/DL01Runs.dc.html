Runs tab

1Only runs. No chips, no “In range”, no “Show all dungeons”: those moved to the Dungeons tab. Groups Today · Tomorrow · Later (headers only when they have runs). A 14:00 run today is in Today.
2Each row: name, time, role pips, “3 in · 2 open”, and your status on the right: you’re in · maybe · can’t · ■ answer · no fit (none of your characters fits; you can still answer).
3Badge on the Runs tab = runs that haven’t started, that you haven’t answered, and where at least one of your characters fits (any character, not only the one you’re on). “No fit” runs never count.
4Bosses appear in the run detail once the run has started: a ✓ per boss from the kill log of anyone in the group (shared live between online addons), and “3 of 6”: optional bosses don’t count (Cookie shows “optional”). The row line becomes “5 in · 3/6 bosses”.
5Dungeon › opens that dungeon in the Dungeons tab (bosses, loot, quests).
6Start passed, no boss yet: the row shows “no boss yet” in grey and stays in Today for 3 hours after the start time, then leaves the list (it stays in the site’s run history). With kills it stays until the daily reset.
7Group rows and Your answer are the existing CF-63 parts, unchanged.
