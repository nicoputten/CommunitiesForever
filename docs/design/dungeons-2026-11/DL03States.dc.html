Rules

1Nothing fits: the group stays with one line, “Nothing fits yet · first at 13”, and Higher lists the rest. Nothing is hidden.
2Quest tag: drops only while you have that quest; the tooltip adds the quest name. These are always shown as usable.
3Recipe drops: “Nobody knows it” when no character in the group has learned it; the tooltip adds who could learn it (has the profession and enough skill). If someone knows it, no tag.
4No runs: “No runs planned” and Find a dungeon ›, which opens the Dungeons tab on Fits you.
5Long lists: 6 per boss, then “2 more ▾”; it expands in place and stays open for that dungeon until /reload.
6no boss yet: start time passed, no kill logged; gone from the list 3 h after the start. no fit: none of your characters fits; not in the badge.
