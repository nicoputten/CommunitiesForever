How answering works

1Every click saves at once. No Save button. The answer goes to friends online with the addon right away; the clock icon shows until the next upload has carried it to the website.
2Character dropdown: all your characters with their fit for this run: “fits”, “carry · +12” (over the range), “4 below”. Picking one updates the role toggles.
3Role toggles: only the roles that class can play (Shaman: Healer, Damage), each with its slot state, “open” or “full”. Picking a full role makes you reserve.
4Note: optional, up to 80 characters, saved when the box loses focus or on Enter.
5Your own row in the group mirrors this: status, character, role and the clock icon. Nobody can change another person’s answer, also not the organiser.
6Works the same for runs planned on the website, in Discord or in the game.
