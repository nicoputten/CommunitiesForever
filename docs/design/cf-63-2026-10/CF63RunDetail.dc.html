Layout decisions

1Group rows get the full width (750 px): role, round class portrait, name, level · friend, then online and the status, right-aligned in a 64 px column. The longest names in the fit check (“Seraphine Dawnwhisper”, “Grimbold Ironhammer”) fit with 140 px to spare.
2Below the group: Your answer (section C), then one row with Online now, fits on the left and Edit or cancel (organiser) or On the website (everyone else) on the right.
3Countdown: “in 1 day”, “in 3 h 10 m”, “in 12 min”, then “Starting now” for 30 minutes after the start; refreshed every minute with C_Timer.NewTicker(60).
4Wings: the list uses short names (SM: Graveyard, SM: Library, SM: Armory, SM: Cathedral); hover shows the full name. The title always uses the full name.
