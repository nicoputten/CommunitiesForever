Tooltips & menus
  1. Celebration banners: A banner when you finish a Doomsday goal or earn a feat.
  2. Celebration sounds: Plays a game sound with your own banners.
  3. Friends’ celebrations: A small line when a friend finishes the day, a weekly or a special feat.
  4. Preview: Shows a sample banner.
Window chrome (title, tabs) not listed.

Behaviour

1All three on by default. “Celebration sounds” only covers the banners; “Play a sound” under Notifications stays separate.
2Celebration banners off: no banners, no sounds; the switch for friends greys out (disabled, still visible).
3Preview shows a medium sample banner for 5 s with its sound, so players can check the volume.
